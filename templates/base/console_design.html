The direction is "instrument panel": a cool porcelain ground, near-white surfaces top-lit so they sit in front of it, one electric lapis blue for anything you can press, one near-black for the surfaces that must out-rank everything around them, and violet reserved for data. Corners are generous, shadows are layered and cool, and every piece of motion is transform/opacity only so the console stays at 60fps on a laptop with twenty tabs open. The light theme is built, not defaulted: the ground carries a faint wash in the console's own two hues and every raised surface is top-lit, because a flat fill next to another flat fill reads as one plane however carefully the neutrals were picked. None of it is applied in dark, where surfaces already separate by lightness and a gradient would only muddy them. Typeface: Inter across the whole console, as everywhere else — drawn to feel designed, humanist enough to read all day, with real tabular figures for tables. JetBrains Mono appears only inside and on machine references (payment ids, API keys), never as UI text. ====================================================================== {% endcomment %}
